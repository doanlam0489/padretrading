FAQs

Questions, answered

What is Padre Trading?

A self-custody terminal for trading crypto onchain, combining execution tools, tracking, and analytics in one interface.

Do I keep control of my funds?

Yes. Padre Trading is non-custodial — your wallet holds the keys, transactions are signed locally, and there's no deposit address to trust.

Which chains are supported?

Solana, Ethereum, Base, and BNB Chain, all switchable from the same screen without changing apps.

What does trading cost?

A small platform fee per executed trade, plus network gas and any slippage — standard costs of trading onchain. Fee cashback offsets part of this automatically.

How does the cashback work?

Every account gets a default cashback rate on trading fees, rising to a higher tier when you sign up through a referral link.

Is there a mobile app?

Padre Trading runs as a installable web app (PWA), so it works like a native app on both desktop and mobile without an app-store download.
