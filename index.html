FAQs

Questions, answered

What is Padre Trading?

A self-custody terminal for trading crypto onchain, combining execution tools, tracking, and analytics in one interface.

Do I keep control of my funds?

Yes. Padre Trading never holds your assets — your wallet, your keys, at every step.

Is there a fee to use it?

A small fee applies per executed trade. There are no charges for browsing data or setting up alerts.

What happens if I lose connection mid-trade?

Orders that are already submitted continue to be processed onchain independently of your connection.

Where can I get help?

Support is available directly in the app, alongside documentation covering every feature in depth.
