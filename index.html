A simple three-part playbook

Not a strategy that predicts price — a structure that keeps you from making avoidable mistakes.

BEFORE

Set limits while you're calm

Position size, daily loss limit, and which presets you'll use — decided before the first trade, not adjusted mid-session under pressure.

DURING

Execute against the plan

Use saved presets and pre-set exit orders instead of re-deciding variables on every single trade while price is moving.

AFTER

Log it before you forget why

A short note per trade — what you saw, what you did — turns a string of trades into something you can actually learn from later.

The discipline side, briefly

Three habits that matter more than any single setting.

Size Before You Search

Decide position size before you start looking for a trade, not after you've already found one you're excited about.

Exits Set in Advance

A stop loss and take profit placed at entry survive a bad moment better than a decision made while the position is moving against you.

A Session Has an End

Deciding your stopping point before you start makes it a plan. Deciding it mid-session, after a string of losses, rarely is.
